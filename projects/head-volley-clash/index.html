§01

Context and Risk

Shipping a full free-to-play title solo means owning every layer at once: the core arcade loop, a balanced virtual economy, seasonal passes and a skin shop, then getting it stable enough to survive real players. With no team to split design, engineering or LiveOps, the challenge was taking it from zero to live on Google Play without any piece falling over.

§04

Outcome

Launched to ~330 daily installs and pulled organic reach across Brazil, Russia, India and Mexico with zero paid marketing, evidence the loop and store presence could acquire players on their own. It still holds ~40 monthly active users despite no updates since release.
