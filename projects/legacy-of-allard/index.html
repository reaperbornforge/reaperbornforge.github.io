§01

Context and Risk

A networked tactical RPG is enormous scope for a small team. Grid-based combat, party synergy and progression all have to hold together before any of them are proven fun. The real risk was sinking years into systems that might never validate, so the goal was to get the core loop playable and technically viable before committing to a full launch.

§04

Outcome

Shipped a browser-playable WebGL alpha with no install friction, backed by PlayFab for accounts and multiplayer, so the grid-combat loop could be tested end to end. A public Steam page went live alongside it to build wishlists and gauge real interest ahead of full production.
